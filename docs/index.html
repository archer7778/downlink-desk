    Audit trail

    Every operator action is a signed entry: it stores a SHA-256 hash of its own content and of the entry before it, so an edited or deleted entry shows up on verify. Alert and pass events are derived from telemetry and can be reproduced at any time, so they are not stored. Tamper-evident, not tamper-proof.

    What Downlink Desk is

    A small operations console for teams who fly or track small things: university CubeSat groups, high-altitude balloon launches, amateur ground stations and early satellite startups. It checks health telemetry against limits, notices when data that should be arriving isn't, keeps ground equipment status in one place, walks operators through each pass, records who did what in a signed log, and turns a shift into a handover report.

    Team · live runs on real time and is shared: everyone with access to this page sees the same spacecraft, alerts, procedure and log, and their actions appear for each other as they happen. Training is private to your browser, with speed control and a separate log, for practice and demos.

    This build runs entirely on simulated data. It has not been verified, validated or qualified for flight or safety-critical use, and must not be relied on to make operational decisions about real hardware.

    Simulated mission

    Spacecraft
    PICO-1, 1U CubeSat, 2S Li-ion battery (2.6 Ah), body-mounted panels
    Orbit
    95 min period, 61 min sunlit / 34 min eclipse
    Downlink
    437.250 MHz, one housekeeping frame every 10 s while in contact
    Ground station
    GS-01, crossed Yagis on an az/el rotator, SDR receiver, mast-head LNA
    Alert rules
    Caution and alarm limits per channel (2 nominal frames to clear), stuck-sensor check (8 identical frames), low array current in sunlight, unexpected reboot, no frames for 60 s during a pass
