Health model

Learns what normal looks like for every point in the orbit, then flags slow drifts that fixed limits miss
ChannelStatusLatest vs normalLast 2 daysTrend / weekSamples

A small satellite warms in sunlight and cools in Earth’s shadow every orbit, so whether a reading is normal depends on where in that cycle it was taken. For each channel the Pi learns the typical value at each point of the cycle (minutes since the satellite last came out of shadow), corrects for longer or shorter shadows through the seasons, and compares the last two days with the three weeks before.     Audit trail

    Download signed log

    Every operator action is signed on the Pi: each entry stores a SHA-256 hash of its content and of the entry before it, so an edited or deleted entry shows up on verify. Alert events are worked out from the telemetry and can be reproduced at any time, so they aren't stored. Tamper-evident, not tamper-proof: someone with access to the Pi's files could rebuild the whole chain.

    What this is

    Downlink Desk is a lightweight operations console running on this Raspberry Pi. Every half hour or so it asks the SatNOGS database for new frames from the satellite, decodes them with the community decoder, and stores them here. The console checks the values against limits, notices when data stops arriving, keeps a signed log of who did what, and builds shift handovers.

    It is not affiliated with the satellite's team and has no way to command the spacecraft. The limits are provisional values chosen for this console, not the mission's own. Do not rely on it for decisions about real hardware.

    Credits

    Telemetry comes from the SatNOGS network run by the Libre Space Foundation and its volunteer station operators. SatNOGS data is shared under the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence. Frames are decoded with satnogs-decoders. Orbit data comes through SatNOGS from public sources. Space weather (geomagnetic Kp index, GOES X-ray flux and 10.7 cm solar flux) comes from NOAA’s Space Weather Prediction Center. The South Atlantic Anomaly outline is an approximation.
